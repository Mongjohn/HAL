      Formats, privacy & Android activation

      Use direct HTTP(S) video files supported by your TV. Playback is muted. Webpage links, HLS/DASH and DRM are not supported. Unplayable videos fall back to artwork. URLs may contain private query tokens and are fetched by the TV.

      Select HAL Artwork in Android TV’s screensaver settings. Android controls activation and sleep. Video settings save separately from dashboard drafts and are not included in settings-file exports.

      Slideshow artwork
